# loomi-platform

Skills that span Bloomreach products (Discovery, Engagement, Data Hub) and use Loomi Connect.

### Running the business

| Skill | What it does | Access |
|---|---|---|
| [weekly-ecommerce-briefing](skills/weekly-ecommerce-briefing/) | One-screen Monday briefing: KPIs, movers, marketing, health alerts and 3 actions | Read-only |
| [qbr-report](skills/qbr-report/) | Quarterly business review: quarter vs last quarter and last year, wins, problems, tests, work done, risks and next quarter's plays | Read-only |
| [revenue-anomaly-explainer](skills/revenue-anomaly-explainer/) | Why revenue or conversion changed: a breakdown that adds up, with causes ranked by evidence | Read-only |
| [peak-readiness-check](skills/peak-readiness-check/) | Go/no-go checklist for Black Friday, sales and launches | Read-only |
| [data-feed-health](skills/data-feed-health/) | Red/amber/green check of the pixel, catalog feeds, Data Hub and Engagement catalogs | Read-only |
| [catalog-parity-check](skills/catalog-parity-check/) | Compares the Discovery and Engagement catalogs: missing products and mismatched price, stock, URL, image and title, ranked by revenue at stake | Read-only |
| [implementation-readiness](skills/implementation-readiness/) | Go-live checklist for a new account: pixel, catalog, attributes, API traffic, core events, IDs, consent and first flows, with an owner for each fix | Read-only |
| [change-impact-tracker](skills/change-impact-tracker/) | Did my change work? Before and after against a control, with a verdict and range | Read-only |

### Planning and growth

| Skill | What it does | Access |
|---|---|---|
| [campaign-planner](skills/campaign-planner/) | Campaign ideas for the next weeks from the retail calendar, last year and current demand (default markets US, UK and EU) | Read-only |
| [collection-suggester](skills/collection-suggester/) | New rule-based collections from search demand and selling products, with live previews | Read-only |
| [search-demand-to-campaign](skills/search-demand-to-campaign/) | Turns Discovery search demand into an Engagement audience and draft campaign | **Writes** |

Skills marked **Writes** only run when you call them by name, and ask for confirmation before every change.
